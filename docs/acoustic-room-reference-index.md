# Garritan Studio — Acoustic Room Research Index

**Research status: 11 November 2026.** This is an acoustic *reference library*, NOT a set of playable reverb presets.

**127 distinct rooms/room collections** · **55 references with published or reported RT values** · All entries have at least one supporting source.

Room names are for research identification and inspiration; there is no affiliation or endorsement. Acoustic measurement conditions, dates, and room configurations differ. `—` means **unknown, not zero**. No room in this index should be presented as an exact impulse-response reconstruction. Historic and closed facilities are intentionally included for archival/acoustic study.

### Evidence terminology

- **published / measured:** published acoustic surveys or technical literature; often historic; check test conditions.
- **studio_reported:** reverb times quoted by venue/operator or instrument provider.
- **acoustic_consultant_estimate / published_estimate / modeled_occupied:** explicitly estimated or model-derived, not necessarily measured with an audience in place.
- **geometry_or_character_documented:** room identity and geometry/description known, no trustworthy room-specific RT assigned.
- **directory_candidate:** location/room appears in a specialist directory; current status and measured acoustics require further research.

### First-choice calibration targets

1. **Abbey Road Studio One:** longer scoring-room sound; design around published midband >2.2 s.
2. **MGM/Sony Stage:** drier classic scoring-room reference around 1.0–1.3 s (historical).
3. **Synchron Stage Vienna A:** controlled 1.6–1.8 s scoring room with low-frequency clarity.
4. **Galaxy Hall:** adjustable 0.8–2.4 s studio reference and documented dimensions.
5. **Skywalker Scoring Stage:** extreme adjustable range 0.6–3.0 s.
6. **Capitol Echo Chambers:** vintage physical acoustic chambers with very long decays.
7. **Boston Symphony Hall / Concertgebouw / Musikverein:** three shoebox concert-hall early-reflection and decay targets.
8. **Walt Disney / Philharmonie de Paris / Elbphilharmonie:** modern surround-hall diffusion targets.
9. **Bayreuth Festspielhaus:** shorter, pit-influenced opera setting.
10. **St Paul's / Notre-Dame:** extended, spatially nonuniform cathedral decays, as experimental presets.

### Reverb DSP implications

- Keep room profiles separate from Studio's native DSP implementation; research records are not selectable presets.
- Prefer spectral RT, early decay time, C80, width/IACC, and impulse/first-reflection timing over single-number RT matching.
- A room's occupied/unoccupied, pre/post-refurbishment, and microphone/source configuration can change values dramatically.
- Variable halls should become *one acoustic space with configurable modes*, rather than unrelated named presets.
- Only use appropriately licensed room impulse measurements for offline calibration; no convolution or third-party DSP at runtime.
- Update references when primary acoustic measurements become available. Preserve a history field for renovations.
