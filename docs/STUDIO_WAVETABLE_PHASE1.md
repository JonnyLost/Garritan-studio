# Studio Synth — Wavetable Phase 1: original native renderer and harmonic editor

Branch: `feature/studio-synth`, draft PR #4 (never merge without authorization).

## Implemented
- **StudioWavetableDSP** is an original C++17 source synthesis engine,
  independent of commercial audio products. Five mathematically generated
  Fourier wavetables: Sine, Triangle, Saw, Square and Glass; plus Custom built
  from 16 user-controlled harmonic amplitudes.
- One shared immutable bank of factory waveforms, 1024 samples each and nine
  bandlimited harmonic tiers (1..256 partials), allocated and generated
  outside the audio callback. 5 x 9 x 1024 x 4 = ~180 KiB shared per process.
  No audio-file disk streaming, sample-library purchases or convolution.
- Two oscillators per voice with independent 0–5 continuous morph positions,
  levels and detuning; up to 16 polyphonic voices, ADSR, velocity-sensitive
  morph, CC1-to-morph, pitch bend, sustain, LFO morph, anti-aliased harmonic
  tier selection, 12-ms morph/gain/harmonic smoothing and audio-frame-offset
  MIDI queue. Shared-bank DSP callback does not allocate or take locks.
- Custom harmonic amplitudes are atomic, copied at render-buffer boundaries,
  then smoothed; no audio-thread table regeneration or waveform allocations.
- Full `StudioWavetablePatch` with 31 stable IDs. Wavetable layers can coexist
  with Analog and FM inside StudioSynthPatch and are persisted per track.
  Existing Analog and FM patches, preset stores and independent engines
  continue to work unchanged.
- Studio Synth panel has a new dedicated Wavetable workspace, interactive
  waveform morph controls and 3D-style spectrum/waveform visualization,
  plus 16 harmonic sliders, layer level/pan and **20 original factory sounds**
  across Keys, Lead, Pad, Bass and Texture.
- Factory sounds are parameter recipes generated by the original DSP; they
  do not include any commercial sample or wavetable data.

## Current limitations / future work
- Live waveform-display animation and oscilloscope capture are *not* yet
  implemented. The vector preview renders modeled wavetable shapes and does
  not claim to read audio-thread buffers.
- Full user wavetable import/export, arbitrary resynthesis, wavetable
  frame drawing and spectral transforms are future additions.
- Wavetable bandlimiting uses harmonic mip tiers, not continuous spectral
  interpolation across frequency; aliasing and level balance require listening.
- Up to four active Studio Synth layers are supported. Global cross-layer
  modulation matrix/shared effects and voice-latency alignment remain work.
- Dedicated factory preset store, MacBook CPU profiling, Mac listening and
  real-device MIDI tests are not yet completed.

## Automated verification
- Linux Swift syntax parsing of all new code.
- Linux AddressSanitizer/UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer standalone rendering,
  morph and harmonic edits, pitch tuning, CC1, pedal, panic and offsets.
- Swift DSP tests; DAWCore wavetable patch/preset regression tests; offline
  AVAudioEngine rendering through StudioSynthInstrument.
- macOS swift build, swift test --parallel and optimized DAWApp build.
